Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how the PyGetPosts application uses data accessed via the Facebook Graph API.

What We Collect

We collect posts and comments from Facebook groups where our app has been added and proper permissions have been granted by users via Facebook Login. We only collect text content and metadata (e.g., timestamps, author IDs).

How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data solely for academic or analytical purposes, including sentiment analysis and topic modeling. No personal identifiers are stored, shared, or displayed publicly.

Data Retention

We do not persist user data beyond the analysis session unless explicitly stated. All processing is conducted in-memory or temporarily for research purposes.

Third Parties

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Your Rights

You may revoke access at any time via your Facebook settings. You may also contact us to request deletion of any stored data.
