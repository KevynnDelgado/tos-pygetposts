Data Deletion Instructions

PyGetPosts does not store any personal data. However, if you wish to remove your Facebook data permissions, follow these steps:

  1. Go to your Facebook Account Settings.
  2. Click on “Apps and Websites”.
  3. Find and select “PyGetPosts”.
  4. Click “Remove”.

This will revoke our access to your data immediately.
