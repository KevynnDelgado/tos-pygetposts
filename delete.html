Data Deletion Instructions

If you wish to delete your Facebook data accessed by PyGetPosts, you can do so in one of the following ways:

Method 1: Via Facebook

  1. Go to your Facebook account settings.
  2. Navigate to “Apps and Websites”.
  3. Find and select “PyGetPosts”.
  4. Click “Remove”. This revokes our access immediately.

Method 2: Contact Us

If you believe any data has been stored and wish to request its deletion, please email us at: delgadokp92@gmail.com
